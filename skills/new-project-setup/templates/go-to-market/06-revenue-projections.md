## Recommended approach to expectations

Set a **learning goal for the first period after launch**, not a revenue goal: get enough
real installs and activated users to know the actual funnel numbers (replacing the ranges
above with real ones). That data is what makes every other doc in this folder something to
iterate on with evidence instead of guesses.
